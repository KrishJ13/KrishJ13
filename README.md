I'm an MSc Computer Science & AI student at Loughborough who likes hard problems, real data, and systems that don't fall over.

to understand disease, find new biomarkers, and speed up how we turn biological data into treatments. Outside of that, I build
data infrastructure, study markets, and tinker with robotics.
